## 📖 The Story Behind PDFSeal & Our Mascot 🦭

Why **PDFSeal**? In English, **"Seal"** has a charming double meaning:
1. **The Security Seal (封条 / 印章)**: Your files are *strictly sealed* inside your local browser memory. Zero bytes ever leak to a cloud server.
2. **The Seal Mascot (可爱小海豹 🦭)**: Meet **Sammy the Seal**, our friendly little document guardian! Sammy ensures your private tax forms, contracts, and receipts remain completely offline and untracked.

> *"Drop your files in, let the little seal stamp and organize them, and leave with zero privacy worries."*

---

## ✨ Features

### 🛠️ Core Local PDF Utilities (100% Offline & Client-Side)
- **Merge PDFs**: Combine multiple documents in any order with intuitive drag-and-drop.
- **Reorder & Rotate**: Visual page thumbnail grid; rotate pages 90°/180°, or delete unneeded pages with one click.
- **Split & Extract**: Select individual pages or custom page ranges (e.g. `1-3, 5`) to export instantly.
- **Watermark & Stamp**: Seal your documents with custom text/image watermarks and adjustable opacity.
- **Metadata Sanitizer**: Strip hidden author names, camera/printer info, creation dates, and edit histories.

### ⚡ Action-Driven AI Tools (BYOK - Bring Your Own Key)
*No bloated chat bots. Pure, fast, deterministic actions.*
- **AI Auto-Rename**: One-click smart renaming based on document contents (e.g., `2026-08_AWS_Cloud_Invoice.pdf`).
- **Table / Receipt to CSV**: Extract structured table data directly into downloadable CSV / Excel format.
- **Smart Redaction**: Auto-detect and black out sensitive data (SSN, emails, phone numbers, total amounts).

---

## 🔒 Privacy Guarantee
- **Zero Server Uploads**: Processing is executed 100% inside your browser using WebAssembly and JavaScript (`pdf-lib` + `pdf.js`).
- **Offline Capable**: You can disconnect your Wi-Fi and all core features will still work flawlessly.
- **Zero Analytics / Tracking**: No cookies, no trackers, no user fingerprinting.
